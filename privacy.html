DIGGSWORTH — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

DIGGSWORTH does not automatically collect, transmit, or share data about you. There is no analytics, advertising, tracking, or account. Information leaves the game only when you deliberately share a run or send a support email.

What the game stores

DIGGSWORTH saves your progress — your bank balance, upgrades, achievements, settings, best depth and career history — on your own device only. On iOS this lives in the app's private storage.

None of it is ever uploaded. We cannot see it, and neither can anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it.

What the game does not do

  • No analytics or telemetry of any kind
  • No advertising, and no ad identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No accounts, logins, or automatic email collection
  • No location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone access
  • No automatic crash or usage reporting

Gameplay makes no network requests and works in airplane mode. Everything in it — including all artwork and sound — ships inside the app. If you choose Report a Bug, DIGGSWORTH asks your device to open an email draft; your mail provider sends it only if you tap Send.

Sharing a run

If you choose to share a shift report, the game hands an image to your device's own share sheet. That is the only time anything leaves the app, it only happens when you tap share, and where it goes is entirely your choice. Nothing is sent anywhere on its own.

Optional bug reports

When you tap Report a Bug, DIGGSWORTH prepares an email containing text you choose to write, the game version and build, language, screen size, and the browser/device description that iOS provides to the app. It never attaches your save, progress, account identifiers, contacts, location, photos, audio, or advertising identifiers. You can review or delete any part of the draft before sending it.

If you send the message, your email address and its contents reach legal@virobit.dev and the email providers used to deliver it. They are used only to reply and diagnose or improve DIGGSWORTH, are not sold or used for advertising, and are deleted when no longer needed for support or legal obligations.

Children

DIGGSWORTH does not automatically collect personal information from anyone, including children. A support email is sent only when a person deliberately writes and sends it using their device's Mail app.

Changes

If a future version changes these data practices or adds automatic collection, this page will say so plainly before that version ships, and the change will be described here rather than buried.

Contact

Questions about this policy: see the support page.
