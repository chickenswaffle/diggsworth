Common questions

The game is silent.
Check the ring/silent switch on the side of your iPhone. DIGGSWORTH asks iOS to play over it, but a Focus mode or a connected Bluetooth device can still take the audio. Sound and music also have their own toggles in the pause menu (P, or the ‖ button).
It's running badly.
Open the settings panel (the ⚙ on the tool strip) and switch Graphics to FAST. That turns off the full-screen shader and cuts particle counts, and it makes a large difference on older devices.
Did I lose my career?
Almost certainly not. Progress is saved to your device's durable storage, specifically so it survives the system clearing out web storage. It does not survive deleting the app, because there is no cloud save — see the privacy policy for why nothing leaves your device.
I'm stuck — I can't afford anything.
Sell everything, then buy the cheapest drill upgrade first; drill speed compounds faster than anything else early. Watch the market board — prices move every time you dock, and holding an ore for one more run is often worth more than selling it now. If the debt clock is the problem, MARROW's directives pay in debt credit rather than cash.
How do I play on a phone without a keyboard?
Swipe anywhere on the play area to drive and drill. The ⚒ button opens the tool strip (dynamite, warp, vac, radar, consoles). Every keyboard action has a touch equivalent.

Reporting a bug

The most useful bug report says what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your depth and how long the run had been going help a lot too. Screenshots are gratefully received.
